## About

[Veridyen Bilişim Teknolojileri San. ve Tic. Ltd. Şti.](https://www.veridyen.com/hakkimizda) was founded in February 2016 in İzmir, Turkey. We provide shared hosting, cloud servers, domains, SSL certificates and the other services a web-based project needs. Our team has worked in the internet service provider sector since 2005.

## Highlights

- Affordable prices and high performance
- Servers in the **MARS data center, Istanbul**, with redundant connectivity through MARS DC and Turknet
- FortiGate IPS/IDS filtering
- **ISO 27001:2022** and **ISO 9001:2015** certified
- 24/7 technical support by ticket and e-mail
- Phone support for billing, sales and technical questions
